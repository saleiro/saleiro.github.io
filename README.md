# Pedro Saleiro

Portuguese AI researcher and entrepreneur. Co-Founder and Chief AI Officer at [Opnova](https://opnova.ai), building AI agents for enterprise operations in regulated industries. Expert in Responsible AI and AI Agents.

Pedro Saleiro has been working at the intersection of AI research and real-world deployment for over a decade. He created the [Aequitas](https://github.com/dssg/aequitas) bias auditing toolkit, one of the most widely adopted open-source tools for AI fairness. Before Opnova, he led the AI Research and FATE (Fairness, Accountability, Transparency, and Ethics) groups at [Feedzai](https://feedzai.com), where his teams filed numerous patents, published at top AI venues (NeurIPS, ICML, KDD, ICLR, AAAI), and shipped multiple research innovations to product. His career includes a postdoctoral position at the [University of Chicago](https://www.uchicago.edu) working with [Rayid Ghani](https://www.rayidghani.com), a Ph.D. internship at [Microsoft Research](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/), and a Ph.D. in Informatics Engineering from the [Faculty of Engineering at the University of Porto](https://sigarra.up.pt/feup/en/web_page.inicial).

## Notable Open-Source Projects

**[Aequitas](https://github.com/dssg/aequitas)** — Bias auditing and AI fairness toolkit used by practitioners, researchers, and policymakers worldwide.

**[TimeSHAP](https://github.com/feedzai/timeshap)** — Model-agnostic explainability method for recurrent neural networks. Published at KDD 2021.

**[FairGBM](https://github.com/feedzai/fairgbm)** — Gradient boosting with fairness constraints. Published at ICLR 2023.

**[Bank Account Fraud Dataset](https://github.com/feedzai/bank-account-fraud)** — First large-scale, privacy-preserving, realistic tabular dataset suite for fraud detection research. Published at NeurIPS 2022.
